Our story, mission & certifications

Our Story

Who We Are

KMP Hyper Foods, founded by food technologists, is a premium maker of ready-to-cook frozen snacks, offering a wide range of products including momos, kebabs, and other innovative items.

Based in Guntur, the spice capital of India, we blend scientific expertise with authentic flavours to create products known for their quality and taste.

Our products are developed by professionals using carefully selected, high-quality ingredients to ensure consistency, safety, and great flavour. Made for modern food businesses, they deliver reliable, restaurant-quality results every time.

With an emphasis on hygiene, innovation and customer satisfaction, KMP Hyper Foods is transforming the way the food industry perceives frozen snacks — making them easy to prepare, delightful to serve, and loved by everyone.

KMP Hyper Foods currently operates across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu.
